Build the Crew Forward Project Plan
Scenario

Your crew has been assigned to plan and deliver a vessel-wide safety and operational readiness initiative that will bring together the bridge, deck, engine, and hotel departments to strengthen coordination and alignment.

The project may include preparing for an inspection, organizing joint drills and briefings, coordinating equipment and materials, and confirming that all departments are ready. Your team is responsible for planning and executing the initiative from start to finish.

Your Task

Create a maritime project plan using the tools from this Deep Dive. Show:

  • What needs to be done onboard
  • When each activity should happen
  • Who is responsible across the crew and departments
Submit as Word, PDF, or clear photos (max 5 pages).
Plan Using These Tools
WBS – Define WHAT must be delivered.
Break the onboard initiative into deliverables, activities, and work packages.
NetPlan – Show WHEN tasks happen.
Identify the sequence, dependencies, milestones, and critical path for safe completion.
RACI – Assign WHO owns each task onboard.
Responsible • Accountable • Consulted • Informed
Tip: If operational details are missing, make reasonable maritime assumptions. A clear, safe, and coordinated plan matters more than a perfect one.
